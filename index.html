Matías Azócar Carvajal

Chilean PhD student in mathematics at Universität Hamburg.

I like (canonical) Ramsey theory, combinatorics and graphs in general.

I am a PhD student at the Department of Mathematics of Universität Hamburg, advised by Mathias Schacht. Most of my work is on canonical Ramsey problems for graphs and hypergraphs.

Before Hamburg, I studied mathematical engineering at Universidad de Chile, where my master’s thesis was on minimum degree conditions for monochromatic cycle partitioning in bipartite graphs.

My papers are listed under Publications, talks and teaching are in my CV, and the blog has things I like to think about. There is also a bit about me.
