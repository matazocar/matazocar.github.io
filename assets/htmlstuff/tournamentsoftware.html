Standard rule: no player's or opponent's win % ever counts as less than this floor when computing tiebreakers, even if their actual win rate is lower. Prevents a single very bad record from unfairly cratering everyone who played them.
How Standings Are Calculated
Players are ranked in this order:
  1. Match Points — 3 per win, 1 per draw, 0 per loss (a bye counts as a win).
  2. Match Win % (MW%) — match points ÷ (rounds played × max points), floored at the configured minimum.
  3. Opponents' Match Win % (OMW%) — the average of each opponent's own MW% (each one individually floored first). Byes are excluded from an opponent's OMW calculation entirely — they're treated as if that round didn't happen for tiebreaker purposes.
  4. Game Win % (GW%) — games won ÷ games played, floored at the same minimum.
  5. Opponents' Game Win % (OGW%) — average of opponents' GW% (floored first, per opponent).
This is where the "weird decimals" come from: the floor means a 0-3 player is never treated as 0% for the purpose of everyone else's OMW% — they're floored up to the configured percentage (default 33.33%) before anyone averages them in. It also means two players can be floored to the identical number even though their raw percentages were different, which is intentional — it's a ceiling on how much one bad matchup can hurt.
