Traitors' Tide

The crew's chest needs filling before payday. Somewhere aboard, mutineers are quietly filling it with cursed coin. Lie well, listen better, and send the right sailors down the plank.

How a voyage works

One friend launches a ship and becomes the captain. The game runs in the captain's browser, so they keep that tab open while you play.

Everyone else opens the captain's invite link, or types the four-letter ship code. Get on a video or voice call: the arguing is half the game.

How to play

3 to 8 players. Each sailor is secretly a pirate or a mutineer. Pirates want the chest to hold enough loot; mutineers want it short.

  • On your turn, do one thing: put a loot card face down in the chest and announce its value (you may lie), or play an action card.
  • Loot: Gold +1, Ballast 0, Cursed coin −2.
  • Plank: give one to another sailor. Three planks and they go overboard, revealing their role.
  • Spyglass: look at the top 3 chest cards and put them back in any order.
  • Shakedown: a sailor of your choice discards their hand and draws 3.
  • Jettison: discard the top 2 chest cards unseen.
  • Net haul: draw 3, then put 2 cards back on the deck in any order. You don't draw at the end of that turn.
  • At the start of their turn, a pirate may reveal themselves and open the chest. If it reaches the goal, pirates win; if not, mutineers win.
  • Pirates also win if every mutineer goes overboard. Mutineers win if they're no longer outnumbered, or if the draw pile runs out.
  • Talk constantly. Ask what people hold. Showing your cards is not allowed.
